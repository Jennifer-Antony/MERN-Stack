Girl with a Pearl Earring

Artist: Johannes Vermeer

Year: 1665

Girl with a Pearl Earring is one of Johannes Vermeer's most famous paintings. It is often called the "Mona Lisa of the North" because of the subject's captivating expression and striking pearl earring.
